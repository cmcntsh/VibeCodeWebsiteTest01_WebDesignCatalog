Image credit standard

Every published visual should identify the image type, subject or site, creator where known, approximate date, source URL or archive, credit line, and rights status. Alt text should describe the design characteristics relevant to the entry rather than merely state that the image is a website screenshot.

Citation policy

Individual trend pages provide a suggested citation for the catalog page and link back to the supplied inventory. Educators and researchers should also cite original archival or primary sources whenever a claim depends on a specific historical website, product, or designer.
