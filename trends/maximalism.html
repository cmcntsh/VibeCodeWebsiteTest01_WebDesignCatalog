Definition

What is Maximalism?

Maximalism emerged partly as a reaction to restrained, highly standardized minimalism. Strong maximalist design still uses hierarchy; it is not simply random clutter.

Visual recognition

Typical elements

Use these recurring characteristics as recognition clues rather than a checklist that every example must satisfy.

  • Dense composition
  • Numerous colors and typefaces
  • Pattern mixing
  • Collage
  • Large decorative elements
  • Layered images and text
  • Animated ornaments
  • Competing focal points
  • Highly individual visual language
  • Deliberate excess
