Definition

What is Neo-Brutalism?

Neo-brutalism combines raw, confrontational composition with bright contemporary branding. Compared with raw HTML brutalism, neo-brutalism is usually meticulously designed despite looking rough.

Visual recognition

Typical elements

Use these recurring characteristics as recognition clues rather than a checklist that every example must satisfy.

  • Thick black outlines
  • Hard offset shadows
  • Flat saturated colors
  • Large block typography
  • Rectangular buttons and cards
  • Minimal gradients
  • Visible borders around most components
  • Intentionally blunt spacing
  • Cartoon-like icons or illustrations
  • High contrast and playful awkwardness
