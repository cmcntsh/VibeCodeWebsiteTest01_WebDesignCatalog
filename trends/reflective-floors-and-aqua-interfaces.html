Definition

What is Reflective Floors and Aqua Interfaces?

Influenced heavily by early-2000s operating-system interfaces, designers simulated polished glass and translucent plastic. This overlaps with Web 2.0 gloss but is more specifically associated with glass, liquid, and polished-plastic surfaces.

Visual recognition

Typical elements

Use these recurring characteristics as recognition clues rather than a checklist that every example must satisfy.

  • Gel-like buttons
  • High-gloss highlights
  • Mirrored logos and images
  • Blue glass textures
  • Translucent panels
  • Rounded capsules
  • Metallic borders
  • Water droplets and light flares
  • Deep shadows beneath floating objects
