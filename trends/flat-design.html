Definition

What is Flat Design?

Flat design removed much of skeuomorphism’s texture and simulated depth. It became broadly popular around 2012. Overly flat interfaces sometimes made clickable elements difficult to recognize because controls lacked clear visual signifiers.

Visual recognition

Typical elements

Use these recurring characteristics as recognition clues rather than a checklist that every example must satisfy.

  • Solid color fields
  • Simple geometric icons
  • Minimal gradients and shadows
  • Sans-serif typography
  • Rectangular or borderless buttons
  • Bright accent palettes
  • Large areas of negative space
  • Emphasis on content over ornament
  • Simplified pictograms
  • Grid-based composition
