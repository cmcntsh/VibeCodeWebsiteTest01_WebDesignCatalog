Definition

What is Portal and Directory Design?

Major services sought to become the user’s starting point for the entire web. Yahoo!, MSN, AOL, and similar services helped establish the portal as a dominant commercial-web format.

Visual recognition

Typical elements

Use these recurring characteristics as recognition clues rather than a checklist that every example must satisfy.

  • Dense multi-column layouts
  • Search box near the top
  • News, weather, finance, sports, email, and shopping modules
  • Extensive category directories
  • Small typography and many hyperlinks
  • Personalized homepage widgets
  • Banner advertising
  • Tabs and boxed content sections
  • High information density above the fold
