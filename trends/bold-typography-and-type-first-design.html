Definition

What is Bold Typography and Type-First Design?

Improved browser font support allowed typography to become a primary visual asset. Some implementations intentionally crop words or let typography extend beyond the viewport.

Visual recognition

Typical elements

Use these recurring characteristics as recognition clues rather than a checklist that every example must satisfy.

  • Extra-large headlines
  • Variable or custom webfonts
  • Dramatic scale contrast
  • Text occupying most of the first viewport
  • Kinetic or animated lettering
  • Unusual line breaks
  • Condensed, extended, or high-contrast display faces
  • Minimal imagery
  • Type integrated into the grid as a graphic shape
