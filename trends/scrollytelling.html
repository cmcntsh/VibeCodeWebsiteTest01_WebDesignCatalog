Scrollytelling

Scrollytelling reveals narrative content as the reader moves through a page.

Main popularity period2012–present

Definition

What is Scrollytelling?

Scrollytelling reveals narrative content as the reader moves through a page. It is common in journalism, data visualization, institutional reports, and product launches.

Visual recognition

Typical elements

Use these recurring characteristics as recognition clues rather than a checklist that every example must satisfy.

  • Sticky graphics beside changing text
  • Maps or charts updating with scroll position
  • Chapter-like sections
  • Sequential animation
  • Full-screen story panels
  • Controlled pacing
  • Progressive data annotation
  • Integrated photography, audio, and video
  • Long-form editorial structure
