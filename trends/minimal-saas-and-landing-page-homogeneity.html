Definition

What is Minimal SaaS and Landing-Page Homogeneity?

A standardized commercial language emerged through design systems, templates, and conversion optimization. It is efficient and familiar but often criticized for making unrelated companies look alike.

Visual recognition

Typical elements

Use these recurring characteristics as recognition clues rather than a checklist that every example must satisfy.

  • Short navigation bar
  • Large benefit-oriented headline
  • Product screenshot in the hero
  • Gradient or abstract background
  • Customer-logo strip
  • Three- or four-column feature section
  • Testimonial cards
  • Pricing table
  • Frequently asked questions
  • Repeated call-to-action buttons
  • Rounded cards and restrained shadows
