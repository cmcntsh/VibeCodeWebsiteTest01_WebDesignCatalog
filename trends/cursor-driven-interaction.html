Definition

What is Cursor-Driven Interaction?

The pointer itself becomes part of the visual experience. Because custom cursors can obscure expected behavior, they are most common on experimental portfolios and campaign sites.

Visual recognition

Typical elements

Use these recurring characteristics as recognition clues rather than a checklist that every example must satisfy.

  • Enlarged circular cursors
  • Labels appearing inside the pointer
  • Cursor changing over links or images
  • Elements magnetically following the pointer
  • Image previews attached to hover
  • Trails, particles, or distortion effects
  • Cursor-controlled 3D movement
